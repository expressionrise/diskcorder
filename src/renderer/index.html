    About Diskcorder

    Diskcorder catalogs your external and offline drives so you can search, browse, and annotate what's on them even when the drive is unplugged. Mapping a drive records every file's name, path, size, and a thumbnail — the files themselves stay where they are.

    Getting started

    • Map a drive — pick a connected drive to scan it into the catalog.
    • Import — load a catalog someone exported as a .json file (its bundled thumbnails come with it).
    • Click a drive in the left rail to browse it; click a file to see details, add a rename label, notes, and tags on the right.
    • Test file for damage (in a file's detail panel) reads the real file off the drive to check it isn't corrupt — ffmpeg decodes videos and images, other files are read end to end to catch bad sectors. Handy for files that won't open.

    The drives rail (left)

    • The top bar shows real disk usage from the system (used of total) when the drive is connected; the details below compare that with how much Diskcorder has cataloged.
    • A blue coverage bar shows how many thumbnails already exist (e.g. 1,240 / 5,678 thumbnails); a gold bar appears below it while a generation pass is running.
    • Update — re-read the connected drive and refresh the catalog: new files appear, deleted ones are removed, and your notes, labels, and tags are kept. Enable Auto-update on connect on a drive to refresh automatically each time it's plugged in.
    • Backup — copy this drive's files to another connected drive. Pick the destination and which top-level folders to include; it copies new and changed files and skips unchanged ones (additive — it never deletes anything at the destination).
    • Relocate — point this drive to a new folder or drive letter and rebuild its catalog (use if its location changed).
    • Previews — generate thumbnail previews for images and videos. Requires your confirmation before starting. Resumes where it left off.
    • Rename — rename the drive's label in Diskcorder only (the disk is untouched).
    • Mark — write an identifier file to the drive so Diskcorder can recognize it when reconnected later.
    • Export — save the catalog as a .json file. Choose to save on your computer or directly on the drive itself (.diskcorder-catalog.json) for seamless workflow.
    • Remove — remove the drive from Diskcorder. The disk and its files are untouched.

    Previews

    • Still previews are taken from the first frame of a video (or the image itself).
    • Previews generate automatically when a drive is connected (and after a scan); generation is resumable — it picks up where it left off and skips anything already made, so re-running is fast.
    • Hover a video (in the list or the detail pane) to play its short preview clip.

    Other views

    • Files — switch between Folders (tree), List (flat filenames + clickable locations), and Gallery (large previews). Sort by name, size, date, label, or tag. Use the folder tree in the left rail to jump to any folder. Long lists load more as you scroll.
    • Starred — everything you've flagged (❤ ● 🚩 ✅) on the open drive, files and folders alike. Click the star on any row, or right-click → flag.
    • Large Files — the biggest files on the drive, filterable by size and modified date, sortable, and optionally grouped by year — to find what's worth clearing or archiving.
    • Space Map — a treemap of what's using room on the drive.
    • Duplicates — find identical files across one or all mapped drives.
    • Test Drive — verify drive health and authenticity. Test a sample of files for corruption and check if the drive's actual capacity matches its claim (detects fake/counterfeit drives with misrepresented capacity).
    • Backup — schedule recurring backup actions to copy files between drives.
    • Search — search names, notes, and labels across every drive (or just the open one).
